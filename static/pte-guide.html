Speaking & Writing

Combined section — approx. 54–67 minutes  |  Tests speaking AND writing skills

1
Read Aloud
Speaking · 6–7 items
▼

A text of up to 60 words appears on screen. After a beep, read the text aloud as naturally and clearly as possible within the time shown on the progress bar.

Preparation Time
30–40 seconds
Recording Time
30–40 seconds
Skills Tested
Speaking + Reading
Scoring
Content, Oral fluency, Pronunciation
💡 Key Tips
  • Do not pause for more than 3 seconds or the recording stops automatically.
  • Maintain a natural pace — don't rush or speak too slowly.
  • Use the prep time to scan and chunk the text.
2
Repeat Sentence
Speaking · 10–12 items
▼

You hear a sentence (3–9 seconds). After the beep, repeat the sentence exactly as you heard it — same words, same order, same tone.

Audio Length
3–9 seconds
Response Time
15 seconds
Skills Tested
Speaking + Listening
Scoring
Content, Oral fluency, Pronunciation
💡 Key Tips
  • Focus on keywords and sentence structure while listening.
  • Even if you miss a word, keep going — don't freeze.
  • Mimicking the speaker's intonation boosts your score.
3
Describe Image
Speaking · 6–7 items
▼

An image (graph, chart, diagram, map, or picture) appears on screen. Describe it in as much detail as possible within the given time.

Preparation Time
25 seconds
Response Time
40 seconds
Image Types
Graph, chart, map, diagram, picture
Scoring
Content, Oral fluency, Pronunciation
💡 Key Tips
  • Use a structured template: intro → main trend → key detail → conclusion.
  • Fill all 40 seconds — don't stop early.
  • Use academic phrases: "The graph illustrates…", "It is evident that…"
4
Re-tell Lecture
Speaking · 3–4 items
▼

You listen to a lecture (60–90 seconds), sometimes accompanied by an image. After the beep, retell the lecture in your own words, covering the key points.

Audio Length
60–90 seconds
Preparation Time
10 seconds
Response Time
40 seconds
Skills Tested
Speaking + Listening
💡 Key Tips
  • Take notes during the lecture — write keywords, not full sentences.
  • Start with: "The lecture discusses…" and cover the main idea + 2–3 details.
  • Speak continuously; avoid long pauses.
5
Answer Short Question
Speaking · 5–6 items
▼

You hear a short question. Give a one-word or very brief answer immediately after the beep. Questions test general knowledge and vocabulary.

Response Time
10 seconds
Expected Answer
1–2 words
Skills Tested
Speaking + Listening
Scoring
Vocabulary (correct word)
💡 Key Tips
  • Listen carefully — the answer is usually one specific word.
  • Build general vocabulary (science, geography, everyday concepts).
  • If unsure, give your best guess — no penalty for wrong answers.
6
Summarize Written Text Writing
Writing · 2–3 items
▼

Read a passage (~300 words) and write a one-sentence summary that captures the main points. Your response must be a single sentence of 5–75 words.

Time Allowed
10 minutes per item
Word Limit
5–75 words (one sentence)
Skills Tested
Reading + Writing
Scoring
Content, Form, Grammar, Vocabulary
💡 Key Tips
  • Identify the topic sentence + 2 key supporting ideas.
  • Use a complex sentence structure with subordinate clauses.
  • Avoid bullet points or multiple sentences — it must be ONE sentence.
7
Write Essay Writing
Writing · 1–2 items
▼

Write a 200–300 word argumentative or discursive essay on a given topic. You may be asked to discuss both sides, give your opinion, or analyze a situation.

Time Allowed
20 minutes per item
Word Limit
200–300 words
Skills Tested
Writing
Scoring
Content, Development, Structure, Grammar, Vocabulary
💡 Key Tips
  • Use a 5-paragraph structure: Intro → Body 1 → Body 2 → Counter → Conclusion.
  • Aim for 280 words — going under 200 or over 380 penalizes you.
  • Avoid repetition; use synonyms and varied sentence structures.
📖

Reading

Approx. 29–30 minutes  |  Tests reading comprehension and vocabulary

1
Reading & Writing: Fill in the Blanks
Reading + Writing · 5–6 items
▼

A passage with several blanks is shown. For each blank, select the most appropriate word from a dropdown list of 4–5 options.

Blanks per Text
4–5 blanks
Skills Tested
Reading + Writing
No. of Options
4–5 per blank
Scoring
1 point per correct blank
💡 Key Tips
  • Read the entire passage first to get context before filling blanks.
  • Look for grammatical clues — the sentence structure narrows choices.
  • Eliminate options that are grammatically or contextually wrong.
2
Multiple Choice, Choose Multiple Answers
Reading · 2–3 items
▼

Read a passage (~300 words) and answer a question by selecting ALL correct responses from a list. There may be more than one correct answer.

No. of Options
5–7 options
Correct Answers
More than one
Negative Marking
Yes — wrong answers deduct points
Skills Tested
Reading
💡 Key Tips
  • Be careful — wrong selections deduct marks.
  • Only select answers that are explicitly stated in the text.
  • Beware of distractors that partially match the text.
3
Re-order Paragraphs
Reading · 2–3 items
▼

Several text boxes containing sentences or paragraphs are shown in random order on the left. Drag and drop them into the correct order on the right to form a coherent passage.

No. of Boxes
4–6 text boxes
Skills Tested
Reading
Scoring
Partial credit for adjacent pairs
Strategy
Identify topic sentence first
💡 Key Tips
  • Find the introductory sentence — it rarely starts with "However" or "Therefore."
  • Look for pronoun references (he, she, it, they) that link to previous sentences.
  • Linking words (furthermore, in contrast, consequently) signal paragraph order.
4
Reading: Fill in the Blanks
Reading · 4–5 items
▼

A passage with blanks is shown. A list of words appears below the passage. Drag the correct word into each blank. There are more words than blanks.

Extra Words
Yes — more options than blanks
Skills Tested
Reading + Vocabulary
Scoring
1 point per correct blank
No Negative Marking
Incorrect ≠ deduction
💡 Key Tips
  • Check the part of speech needed for each blank (noun/verb/adjective).
  • Use collocations — certain words naturally go together (e.g., "significant impact").
  • Start with the blanks you're most confident about.
5
Multiple Choice, Choose Single Answer
Reading · 1–2 items
▼

Read a passage and answer a question by selecting the single best response from 4–5 options. Only one answer is correct.

No. of Options
4–5
Correct Answers
Exactly one
Negative Marking
No
Skills Tested
Reading
💡 Key Tips
  • Use the process of elimination — cross out clearly wrong options.
  • Base your answer only on the text — don't use outside knowledge.
  • Watch for paraphrased options — the correct answer may reword the text.
🎧

Listening

Approx. 30–43 minutes  |  Tests listening comprehension and note-taking

1
Summarize Spoken Text
Listening + Writing · 2–3 items
▼

Listen to a lecture or talk (60–90 seconds). Write a 50–70 word summary covering the key points. You have 10 minutes per item.

Audio Length
60–90 seconds
Time Allowed
10 minutes
Word Limit
50–70 words
Scoring
Content, Form, Grammar, Vocabulary, Spelling
💡 Key Tips
  • Take notes during the audio — keywords and figures are critical.
  • Paraphrase the audio; don't copy phrases verbatim.
  • Check word count — too short or too long affects the Form score.
2
Multiple Choice, Choose Multiple Answers
Listening · 2–3 items
▼

Listen to a recording and select ALL correct answers from a list. Multiple correct options may exist. Negative marking applies.

Negative Marking
Yes
Skills Tested
Listening
Audio Type
Interview, lecture, discussion
Played
Once only
💡 Key Tips
  • Read the question and options BEFORE the audio plays.
  • Don't select options that sound familiar but aren't directly stated.
  • If in doubt between 2 options, don't guess randomly — negative marking hurts.
3
Fill in the Blanks
Listening + Writing · 2–3 items
▼

A transcript with blanks appears on screen. Listen to the audio and type the missing words into the blanks. The words must be spelled correctly.

Skills Tested
Listening + Writing
Spelling
Must be exact — spelling counts
Audio
Played once
Scoring
1 point per correct blank
💡 Key Tips
  • Read the transcript quickly before the audio — predict possible words.
  • Focus on the blanks while listening; don't get distracted by the rest.
  • Double-check spelling — one wrong letter = zero points for that blank.
4
Highlight Correct Summary
Listening · 2–3 items
▼

Listen to a recording (~90 seconds). Read several paragraphs and select the one that best summarizes the recording.

No. of Options
4–5 summaries
Skills Tested
Listening + Reading
Scoring
1 point (all or nothing)
Audio
Played once
💡 Key Tips
  • Skim the summaries before the audio plays.
  • Eliminate options with facts not mentioned in the recording.
  • The correct summary won't add outside information.
5
Multiple Choice, Choose Single Answer
Listening · 1–2 items
▼

Listen to a recording and answer a question by selecting the single best response from 4–5 options. No negative marking.

Negative Marking
No
Skills Tested
Listening
Correct Answers
Exactly one
Audio
Played once
💡 Key Tips
  • Read the question before listening to know what to focus on.
  • Trust what you heard — don't second-guess based on assumptions.
  • Since there's no penalty, always select your best guess.
6
Select Missing Word
Listening · 2–3 items
▼

Listen to a recording where the last word or phrase is replaced by a beep. Select the most appropriate option to complete the audio from the list provided.

No. of Options
4–5
Skills Tested
Listening
Negative Marking
No
Played
Once
💡 Key Tips
  • Pay attention to the tone and context of the whole recording.
  • The correct word must fit logically AND grammatically.
  • Often tests prediction and understanding of context.
7
Highlight Incorrect Words
Listening · 2–3 items
▼

A transcript appears on screen while you listen to the audio. Click on the words in the transcript that differ from what the speaker actually says.

Skills Tested
Listening + Reading
Scoring
+1 correct click / −1 wrong click
Audio
Played once
Incorrect Words
2–7 differences
💡 Key Tips
  • Follow the transcript word by word as you listen.
  • Differences are usually single word substitutions, not added/removed words.
  • Don't click unless you're sure — wrong clicks reduce your score.
8
Write from Dictation
Listening + Writing · 3–4 items
▼

You hear a short sentence (spoken once). Type the sentence exactly as you heard it. Spelling and punctuation must be accurate.

Sentence Length
~10–15 words
Skills Tested
Listening + Writing
Scoring
1 point per correct word
Audio
Played once
💡 Key Tips
  • Type as you listen — don't wait until the audio finishes.
  • Spelling must be perfect; each correctly typed word = 1 point.
  • If you miss a word, guess based on context rather than leaving it blank.
